Stela keeps the things you need to remember where you will actually see them: pinned to your status bar as ongoing notifications.

Write a plain note, pin it, and it stays in your notification shade until you unpin it. No folders, no sync, no account - just the notes that matter, in front of you.

FULLY OFFLINE

Stela does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send your notes anywhere, even by accident. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no sign-in.

WHAT IT DOES

- Notes as notifications - pin any note as an ongoing status-bar notification.
- Persistent by design - pinned notes re-post themselves if you swipe them away, survive a reboot, and resist being killed in the background.
- Capture from anywhere - a quick-add notification, a home-screen widget, launcher shortcuts and a Quick Settings tile each open a small popup that floats over whatever you are doing.
- Pin later, unpin later - schedule a note to pin at a chosen time, have it unpin itself afterwards, or snooze a pinned note to bring it back when you need it.
- Archive - set notes aside without deleting them, then restore or remove them later.
- Organise - search, sort and filter your notes, and give each one its own emoji.
- Share into Stela - send text from another app straight into a new note.
- Backup - export and import your notes as a JSON file, entirely on your own device.
- Themes - light, dark or follow the system, with Material You colours.

HONEST ABOUT ANDROID

Modern Android cannot guarantee a notification that can never be dismissed, or an app that can never be stopped. Stela promises what it can actually deliver: pinned notes re-post themselves if cleared, come back after a reboot, and resist background kill. A short setup flow points you at the battery settings that make this reliable on phones that are aggressive about stopping apps.
